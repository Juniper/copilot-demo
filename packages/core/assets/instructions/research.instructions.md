## 1. Research Instructions

- Understand the problem deeply. Carefully read the issue and think critically about what is required.
- Ask the user for other relevant publications or resources they are aware of.
- Summarize findings and propose potential solutions or areas for further exploration.
- Explain in detail the rationale behind each proposed solution and how it addresses the problem at hand.
- Do a comparative analysis of the proposed solutions, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses.

## 2. Ideation

- Your thinking should be thorough. 
- It is absoluly fine if you have to go through multiple iterations of your process. 
- You should be concise, but thorough.
- Your ideas should be well-organized and clearly articulated.
- Consider using visual aids or diagrams to represent complex ideas.
- You can explore unconventional solutions or approaches.
